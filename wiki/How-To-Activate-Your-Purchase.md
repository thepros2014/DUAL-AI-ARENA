## 📬 1. Receiving Your License Key

When you purchase **DUAL-AI-ARENA** on Payhip:
1. Payhip immediately displays your **Order Confirmation Screen** with your license key.
2. You will also receive an **Order Receipt Email** from Payhip containing your key.
3. Your License Key format looks like:
   ```
   DUALAI-ENT-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
   ```

## 💻 2. Activating Inside the Application

1. Launch **DUAL-AI-ARENA** on your Windows machine.
2. Click the **"FREE TRIAL"** badge in the top-right corner of the header (or wait for the purchase screen after completing 2 free trial turns).
3. Paste your license key into the **Enterprise License Key** input field.
4. Click **"ACTIVATE KEY"**.
5. The application will instantly:
   - Verify your license key.
   - Encrypt your license state using **AES-256-GCM + Windows DPAPI**, binding it to your local machine.
   - Unlock **Unlimited AI Battles**, multi-model consensus, and all enterprise features.

## 🔒 3. Air-Gapped & Offline Activation

If your development workstation or corporate environment has **no internet access (100% air-gapped)**:
- **No problem!** The application validates your key locally on your device without making outbound network requests.
- Zero telemetry, zero external tracking.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Customer Questions

### Q: Can I use my key on multiple computers?
**A**: Your license key can be activated on your personal development machines. Each machine will encrypt its own local license record.

### Q: What if I lose my license key?
**A**: Check your original Payhip purchase confirmation email, or contact support with your purchase email at **[thepros2014@gmail.com](mailto:thepros2014@gmail.com)** to have your key re-sent.
