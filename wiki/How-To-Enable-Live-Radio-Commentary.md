# 🎙️ How to Enable Live Radio Voice Commentary
**Play-by-Play Audio Synthesis, Speech Rate & Persona Settings**

---

## 📻 1. Activating the Live Radio Commentator

1. In the Arena match interface, look at the **Radio Commentary HUD** in the bottom panel.
2. Click the **"Live Radio: OFF"** button to toggle it to **"Live Radio: ON"** (Green active state).
3. Ensure your computer's audio output is unmuted.

---

## 🗣️ 2. What the Commentator Announces
- **Kickoff Possession**: Announces the start of the match and which model has offensive initiative.
- **Vulnerability Alerts**: Calls out newly detected CWE vulnerabilities (e.g. *"Red Team has landed a critical timing attack on auth.js!"*).
- **Patch Deployments**: Calls out when Blue Team successfully releases a cryptographic remediation.
- **Consensus Verdicts**: Announces final multi-model agreement scores.

---

## 🎚️ 3. Audio Controls & Volume
- Adjust the volume slider or mute toggle in the Radio HUD.
- The commentator runs completely client-side using the local browser Web Speech synthesis API with zero audio latency.
