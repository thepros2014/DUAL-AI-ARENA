# Play-by-play commentary and audio controls

The **PLAY-BY-PLAY** button is in the top navigation bar. It controls optional spoken commentary while you follow the run on screen. Labels below match the current application source.

---

## 1. Turn commentary on

1. Check that Ollama and the local commentator model are available in your installation.
2. Find **PLAY-BY-PLAY OFF** in the top navigation bar.
3. Click it and confirm that the label changes to **PLAY-BY-PLAY ON AIR**.
4. Run a small practice turn and follow the commentary alongside the visible activity.
5. Click the button again to switch speech off.

Use Windows audio controls to select your output device and adjust volume. The top-bar button is an on/off control, not a volume or speech-rate slider.

---

## 2. Understand the status labels

| Label | Meaning | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PLAY-BY-PLAY OFF** | Speech is available but switched off. | Click to enable it. |
| **PLAY-BY-PLAY ON AIR** | Speech is enabled. | Listen during run activity; click to switch off. |
| **SIDELINE OFFLINE** | The local commentator is unavailable. | Check the installed commentator model and Ollama setup. |
| **AUDIO UNAVAILABLE** | The app lacks audio support or cannot find a suitable voice. | Hover over the control for its explanation and check your Windows voice setup. |

The control may request a Windows Natural or Online English voice. When speech is unavailable, use the visual commentary and event displays to follow the run.

---

## 3. Check silent or delayed audio

- If the control is unavailable, resolve the reason in its tooltip before retrying.
- If it says **ON AIR** but you hear nothing, check the Windows volume mixer, selected output device and mute state, then observe a turn with new activity.
- Spoken commentary may arrive after the corresponding event. Use the visible turn status and results when deciding what to do next.

Commentary describes the run; it does not independently verify a finding or proposed fix. Inspect and test results using [[Tutorial-Review-and-Test-a-Patch]].
