# 🚀 DUAL-AI-ARENA — Quick Start Guide
**Step-by-Step Installation, First Launch, Digital EULA & Battle Execution**

---

## 1. Installation

Choose any of the three distribution options:
- **Option A (Recommended)**: Double-click `DualAI-MID-Arena-Setup.exe` (or `DualAI-MID-Arena-Free-Setup.exe`) and click **"Install Now"**.
- **Option B (Installer ZIP)**: Extract `DualAI-MID-Arena-v1.0.0-Windows-Installer.zip` and run `Setup.bat`.
- **Option C (Portable Standalone)**: Extract `DualAI-MID-Arena-v1.0.0-Windows-Portable.zip` and double-click `DualAI.Arena.exe`.

> [!TIP]
> **Process Termination Guarantee**: When you click `[X]` on the native window, all background processes and worker threads terminate instantly.

---

## 2. First Launch & Digital EULA Sign-Off

Upon launching for the first time:
1. Review the commercial software license and anti-piracy notice.
2. Enter your Company/Name, Email, and digital signature.
3. Click **"ACCEPT, DIGITALLY SIGN & ENTER ARENA →"**.
4. The agreement is signed and stored in the application's protected local storage.

---

## 3. Starting Your First Match

1. In the **Create Challenge** screen, enter a local project folder or paste source code.
2. Ensure at least one AI model provider is toggled on (OpenAI, Gemini, or local Ollama).
3. Click **"Start Match & Enter Arena →"**.
4. Toggle **"AUTO HANDOFF READY"** to let Red Team and Blue Team battle autonomously, or use manual turn stepping!
