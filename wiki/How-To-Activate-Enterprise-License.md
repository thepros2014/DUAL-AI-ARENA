# 🔑 How to Activate an Enterprise License Key
**Upgrading from Free Trial, Payhip Checkout & In-App Activation**

---

## 🛒 1. Purchasing an Enterprise License Key

1. Visit the official Payhip store at **[https://payhip.com/b/Tfz7D](https://payhip.com/b/Tfz7D)**.
2. Select your desired license tier (Enterprise Pro, Developer Open-Integration Full Source, or Business SLA).
3. Complete checkout to receive your license key (Format: `DUALAI-ENT-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`).

---

## 💻 2. Activating Your Key In-App

1. Launch DUAL-AI-ARENA.
2. Click the **"FREE TRIAL"** badge in the topbar, or wait for the purchase screen after completing 2 free trial turns.
3. Paste your license key into the **License Key** field.
4. Click **"ACTIVATE KEY"**.
5. The key is verified and stored in the application's protected local storage, unlocking unlimited battles.
