## 📌 1. How License Activation Works

When customers purchase **DUAL-AI-ARENA** on Payhip, license keys are automatically generated and delivered via order confirmation email and on-screen. The key is verified and stored securely on your local machine using **AES-256-GCM + Windows DPAPI** — no data is sent to external servers during activation.

---

## 🛒 2. Completing Your Purchase

1. Visit the official store at **[https://payhip.com/b/Tfz7D](https://payhip.com/b/Tfz7D)**.
2. Select your desired license tier.
3. Complete checkout — your license key is displayed immediately on the confirmation screen and emailed to you.

Your key format will look like:
```
DUALAI-ENT-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
```

---

## 💻 3. Activating Your License In-App

1. Launch **DUAL-AI-ARENA**.
2. Click the **"FREE TRIAL"** badge in the topbar (or wait for the upgrade prompt after 2 trial turns).
3. Paste your license key into the **Enterprise License Key** field.
4. Click **"ACTIVATE KEY"**.
5. Your license is verified and securely stored locally — unlimited battles unlock instantly.

---

## 🔒 4. Air-Gapped & Offline Activation

If your environment has **no internet access (100% air-gapped)**:
- The application validates your key locally without any outbound network requests.
- Zero telemetry, zero external tracking.
