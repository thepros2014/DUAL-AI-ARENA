## Ask a useful support question

Use a title that describes the problem, such as “Model setup stops before my first review.” Include:

- App version and operating system.
- What you were trying to do.
- Steps that reproduce the issue.
- Expected result and actual result.
- A short, sanitized error message, if available.

Remove keys, license information, customer data, private paths, and source code you cannot publish. Keep purchase-specific information out of public threads and use the support contact provided with your purchase.

## Suggest a feature

Describe the problem, who it affects, your current workaround, and what a successful result would look like. Link an existing discussion when the idea has already been raised. Roadmap items describe plans, not delivery commitments.

## Share a result

Describe your workflow, model selection, and what you checked yourself. Use synthetic or publishable examples. AI findings and proposed changes require review and testing before use.

Read the [community rules](DISCUSSION_RULES.md) before posting. Keep threads focused, respectful, and useful to other customers.
